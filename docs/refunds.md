# Refunds

## Standard refunds
Customers can return unused items within 30 days of delivery for a full
refund to the original payment method. A receipt or order number is required.

## Sale items
Items bought at a discount of 40% or more are final sale and cannot be
refunded. They can still be exchanged for a different size within 14 days.

## Damaged on arrival
If an item arrives damaged, the customer gets a full refund or a free
replacement, and we pay return shipping. Photos must be sent within 7 days.

## Refund timing
Refunds are issued within 5 business days after the returned item reaches
our warehouse. Bank processing can add up to 5 more days.
