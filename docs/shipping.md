# Shipping

## Delivery times
Orders ship within 2 business days. Delivery inside the EU takes 3 to 5
business days. Delivery to Switzerland and Norway takes 5 to 8 business days.

## Shipping costs
Shipping is free for orders over 80 euros. Below that, it costs 5.90 euros.

## Return shipping
Return shipping costs 4.50 euros and is deducted from the refund, unless the
item arrived damaged or we sent the wrong item.
